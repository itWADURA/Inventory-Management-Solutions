# User Guide - Itwadura Inventory

A calm, practical inventory management system designed for small shops, workshops, cafés, schools, and offices.

---

## 1. Quick Start Workflow

### Step 1: Sign Up & Create Your Workspace
1. Navigate to the signup page.
2. Enter your business name (e.g. *Downtown Hardware* or *Metro Café*) and your account details.
3. Your initial workspace and default **Main location** are automatically initialized.

### Step 2: Add Your First Item
1. Click **Items & Catalog** in the sidebar, then select **Add New Item**.
2. Enter the item name, optional item code (SKU), unit of measure (e.g. piece, box, kg), and reorder alert level.
3. If you already have physical units on hand, enter them under **Starting Stock (Optional Opening Balance)**.

### Step 3: Daily Stock Movements
Use the quick action buttons on the Dashboard or in the top navigation:
- **Add Stock (+):** Record arrivals from suppliers, deliveries, or returns.
- **Use Stock (-):** Record items sold, consumed in repairs, or discarded.
- **Adjust Count (&plusmn;):** Conduct a physical shelf audit. Enter the exact count you see and provide a verified reason.

## 3. Installing as a Progressive Web App (PWA)

Itwadura Inventory can be installed as an app on your phone, tablet, or desktop:

- **Chrome / Edge (Desktop or Android):**
  Click the **Install** icon in the address bar (or select menu &rarr; *Install Itwadura Inventory*).
- **Safari (iPhone / iPad):**
  Tap the **Share** button at the bottom of the screen, scroll down, and tap **Add to Home Screen**.

Once installed, Itwadura launches in a clean, standalone fullscreen window without browser chrome.
